## meETHZ

At university, especially in the first year, people often don't have other people to study with. That's why we built a solution to that problem. A web application where you can either schedule a study session and other people can join it so that you can study together and meet new people, or you spontaneously join another session if your friends don't have time, for example. Beyond that, you can also show the shortest path from one room to another on a map, and most importantly, the way to the nearest toilet :). Go meet to study or study to meet new people.
